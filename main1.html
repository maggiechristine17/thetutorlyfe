For parents

Why parents can trust The Tutor Lyfe Practice

The Tutor Lyfe Practice gives your child unlimited, standards-aligned math practice with instant feedback, the same proven approach used by leading platforms like IXL. The difference: every student's practice is reviewed by a real, certified tutor who uses it to plan their next lesson.

It was designed by Maggie, a certified Georgia teacher in mathematics and special education with a master's degree in special education, together with Joe, an engineer with an MBA.

How it works

Students practice one skill at a time until they truly master it, not just until they finish a worksheet.

  • A mastery score from 0 to 100. Each correct answer raises the score; each miss lowers it. Near the top, gains get smaller and misses cost more, so a student can only reach 100 by answering correctly again and again.
  • An explanation after every miss. Students see the right answer and the steps to get there before moving on. Mistakes become lessons.
  • Unlimited fresh questions. Questions are generated new each time, so students can't memorize answers.
  • Aligned to the standards taught in school. Skills follow grade-level standards, starting with 4th grade place value, rounding, addition, and subtraction.
  • Progress you can see. Each skill shows the student's best score and a medal once it's mastered.

What a real tutor adds

Practice apps show a score; your child's tutor turns that score into a plan.

  1. Your child practices at home, at their own pace.
  2. The tutor reviews the results, including a report that maps each answer to the specific state standards your child has mastered and the ones they are still working on.
  3. Live sessions target the gaps. The tutor spends session time on the exact skills that need it.
  4. You get clear updates in plain language: what improved, what's next, and how you can help at home.

Questions parents ask

Is this connected to IXL?No. The Tutor Lyfe built its own practice platform and writes its own questions. We mention IXL only because many families know it. How much should my child practice?Short, regular sessions work best. Your tutor will suggest a weekly goal that fits your child. Who can see my child's results?Only your child's tutors at The Tutor Lyfe. Does it replace live tutoring?No. Practice builds fluency between sessions; live tutoring builds understanding. Together they move faster than either alone.
